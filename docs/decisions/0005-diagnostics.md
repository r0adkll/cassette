# 0005: Diagnostics

Date: 2026-10-16 · Status: accepted

## Decision

Diagnostics are a first-class area reached from Settings, present in every build. They hold raw adverts,
nearby SRAM-looking devices, hub table dumps, ride tests and scan profiles. Component screens link into it.

## Why

The protocol is reverse engineered and validated on one bike. Supporting new hardware or firmware changes
depends on these tools being available on any install, including other people's if the app goes public.
