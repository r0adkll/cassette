# 0004: Alerts

Date: 2026-10-16 · Status: accepted

## Decision

v1 notifies when a component's status changes to low or charge now (once per change), with a pre-ride nudge
when the bike wakes and something is already low, and a reminder when a bike hasn't been seen for a while.

Settled since: the stale-data thresholds (14 and 30 days) are in [0010](0010-product-details.md); channels
and behaviour are in [0011](0011-onboarding-alerts-and-data.md). Notification ids go in the ledger in CLAUDE.md.
