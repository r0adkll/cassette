Never ride single-speed by accident. Cassette shows the battery of every component on your bike (derailleurs, shifters, power meter) on your iPhone, your Apple Watch and your Lock Screen.

Works with SRAM AXS wireless components. Cassette is an independent app: it is unofficial and isn't made, endorsed or supported by SRAM. SRAM and AXS are trademarks of SRAM, LLC.

HOW IT WORKS
• Wake the bike near your iPhone: a shake or a shift is enough. Cassette hears the components' Bluetooth broadcasts and reads the rest from the rear derailleur. No pairing, no account, no other app open.
• Each component shows the status it reports (good, low or charge now), with a percentage where it gives one and a marked ≈ estimate from its voltage where it doesn't.
• Several bikes? Add each one and switch between them.

BEFORE THE RIDE
• A heads-up when the bike wakes and something is already low.
• An alert when a component drops to low or charge now.
• A reminder when a bike hasn't been seen for a while.

EVERYWHERE YOU LOOK
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and StandBy.
• A Live Activity in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen while a refresh runs.
• Apple Watch: a page for each bike, complications and a Smart Stack card, kept in step with your iPhone.
• iPad: a dashboard with your bikes beside it.

HISTORY
• 30-day, 90-day and 1-year charts for each component, marking charges and coin-cell swaps, beside its voltage, shift count and firmware.
