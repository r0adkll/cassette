Never ride single-speed by accident. Cassette shows the battery of every component on your bike (derailleurs, shifters, power meter) on your phone, your watch and your home screen.

Works with SRAM AXS wireless components. Cassette is an independent app: it is unofficial and isn't made, endorsed or supported by SRAM. SRAM and AXS are trademarks of SRAM, LLC.

HOW IT WORKS
• Wake the bike near your phone: a shake or a shift is enough. Cassette hears the components' Bluetooth broadcasts and reads the rest from the rear derailleur. No pairing, no account, no other app open.
• Each component shows the status it reports (good, low or charge now), with a percentage where it gives one and a marked ≈ estimate from its voltage where it doesn't.
• Several bikes? Add each one and switch between them.

BEFORE THE RIDE
• A heads-up when the bike wakes and something is already low.
• An alert when a component drops to low or charge now.
• A reminder when a bike hasn't been seen for a while.

EVERYWHERE YOU LOOK
• Home screen widgets for one bike or all of them.
• A Quick Settings tile.
• Wear OS: your bikes on the watch, a tile and complications, kept in step with the phone.
• Layouts for foldables and tablets.

HISTORY
• 30-day, 90-day and 1-year charts for each component, marking charges and coin-cell swaps, beside its voltage, shift count and firmware.

PRIVATE
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. What Cassette reads stays on your devices.

WORTH KNOWING
• The Bluetooth protocol was worked out independently and has been tested on one road bike so far. Other wireless groupsets should work; anything Cassette doesn't recognise shows up in Diagnostics, where you can share a report.
• Cassette is open source: github.com/r0adkll/cassette
